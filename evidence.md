# Evidence: Key Insights from User Research

## Sources

| ID | Source | Role | Context |
|----|--------|------|---------|
| S1 | Interview: Louise Jäger, 26 Oct 2026 | Global Logistics, Novo Nordisk (IT integrations of external warehouses) | External warehouses, strategic view |

## Key Insights

### 1. Shipment tracking is manual
Warehouse operators
want to record each shipment's movement through inbound, storage, sampling and distribution
because every batch must be traceable to its serial numbers and official batch records,
but this is largely done by hand, which is time-consuming and hard to track.
*Sources: S1, S2*

### 2. Sample testing adds a manual step
Warehouse operators
want to log that a sample was taken from every shipment and track its test status
because each shipment requires sample testing before it can move on,
but this adds another step to an already manual process.
*Source: S2*

### 3. Efficiency cannot be measured
Warehouse managers
want to measure warehouse efficiency
because they need it for capacity planning and budgeting,
but nothing is recorded digitally, and even at corporate level the efficiency benefits of digitalisation were not quantified.
*Sources: S1, S2*

### 4. Inbound and outbound areas overfill
Warehouse managers
want to keep goods flowing through the inbound and outbound areas
because overfilled areas block the general flow of the warehouse,
but there is no data showing when and why they overfill.
*Source: S2*

### 5. Delivery timing is guesswork
Warehouse managers
want to schedule deliveries when the warehouse can handle them most efficiently
because this avoids traffic at the docks,
but without quantitative data they cannot plan delivery times.
*Source: S2*

### 6. Compliance comes first
Logistics decision-makers
want to prioritise digital projects
because compliance is their "license to operate",
but pharma adopts new logistics technology only after it is proven.
*Source: S1*

## Implications for the Product

| Insight | Feature |
|---------|---------|
| 1 | QR scan per shipment, linked to serial numbers and batch records, tracked through inbound, storage, sampling and distribution |
| 2 | Sampling step with test status (pending, passed, failed) for every shipment |
| 3 | Efficiency dashboard: time per step and per shipment, for capacity planning and budgeting |
| 4 | Live view of inbound and outbound area fill levels |
| 5 | Delivery and traffic patterns over time to plan delivery slots |
| 6 | Full audit trail of every scan, designed around compliance requirements |
