# Evidence-to-feature rationale

| Research / product insight | Prototype feature | Demonstration |
|---|---|---|
| Manual operational recording creates administrative effort | Record activity | Operator records a shipment stage in a few taps |
| Supervisors need visibility of waiting work and delays | Monitor shipment list | Delayed shipments are marked after four hours |
| Issues can be difficult to communicate and follow up | Issue reporting and resolution | Operator reports; supervisor resolves |
| Receiving and dispatch areas can become congested | Area-fill indicator | Monitor shows fictional capacity |
| Managers need measurable operational information | Dashboard metrics | Activities, delays, and issues are aggregated |
| Timing and capacity planning matter | Hourly chart and 7-day trend | Dashboard shows busy periods and change over time |
| SAP remains the system of record | Simulated SAP page | Sending → Sent or Not sent → Retry |

All evidence is preliminary and must remain linked to anonymised interviews in evidence.md. Do not present prototype values as measured real-world outcomes.
